Welcome to Study Nest! 
Study Nest is a powerful, offline AI-powered educational app designed to help you study Math, English, and more. Because the AI models are built directly into the app, you do not need an internet connection to use it.

📖 FEATURES

· Offline AI: Uses the Qwen 2.5 AI model to answer your questions without needing Wi-Fi.
· Built-in Resources: Comes pre-loaded with Math and English materials.
· Privacy First: All your conversations and notes stay on your computer. No data is sent to the cloud.
· Portable & Easy: No complex setup required.

💻 SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

To ensure the app runs smoothly, your computer should meet these minimum requirements:

· OS: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
· RAM: 8 GB or more (16 GB is strongly recommended for AI processing)
· Storage: At least 5 GB of free disk space
· Processor: Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3 or better

📥 HOW TO OPEN THE APP

If you received a ZIP file:

1. Extract the ZIP: Right-click the downloaded .zip file and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the folder: Go into the newly extracted folder.
3. Do not run the app while it is still inside the ZIP file, as it will crash.

If you received a folder (win-unpacked):

1. Open the folder.
2. Find the file named Study Nest.exe.
3. Double-click it to start the app.

If you received a single file (Study Nest 1.0.0.exe):

1. Simply double-click the file to open the app.

(Note: Because this app contains large AI models, it might take 1 to 3 minutes to open the very first time. Please be patient while it loads the AI into memory).

⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNINGS & TROUBLESHOOTING

1. "Windows protected your PC" (Blue Box)
Because this is a custom-made app, Windows SmartScreen might block it.

· Fix: Click "More info" on the blue box, then click "Run anyway".

2. Antivirus Blocking the App
Some antivirus software flags new, large .exe files. This is a false alarm.

· Fix: Check your antivirus notifications and select "Allow" or "Trust" for Study Nest.

3. The App is Stuck on a Blank Screen

· Fix: Wait 2 full minutes. The AI model is loading into RAM. If it takes longer, close the app (End Task in Task Manager) and try opening it again.

💾 WHERE ARE MY NOTES SAVED?

If you add new Math or English resources inside the app, they are saved safely in your Windows User folder (AppData).

· If you update the app later, your notes will not be lost.
· If you want to back up your notes or move them to another computer, press Windows Key + R, type %appdata%, and copy the Study Nest folder.

🗑️ HOW TO UNINSTALL

Since this is a portable app, you don't need an uninstaller.

1. Delete the Study Nest folder or .exe file you downloaded.
2. (Optional) Delete the Study Nest folder inside %appdata% to remove all saved notes and settings.
